Academic career

Tianshi Chen is a professor in the School of Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. From April 2009 to December 2015, he worked in the Division of Automatic Control in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Linköping University, Sweden, first as a postdoctoral researcher and then as an assistant professor from April 2011.

He returned to China and joined CUHK-Shenzhen as an associate professor in December 2015. He received tenure in July 2018 and became a professor in July 2023.

Research

He develops data-driven and physics-informed methods, algorithms, and theory for modeling, control, and decision-making. His work combines machine learning, statistical theory, and efficient numerical algorithms, with applications in control and spatiotemporal data modeling.

  • Data-driven modeling

    Kernel methods in reproducing kernel Hilbert spaces, regularized system identification, Bayesian inference, and deep learning.

  • Data-driven control and decision-making

    Data-driven model predictive control, stochastic adaptive control, iterative learning control, online decision-making, and reinforcement learning.

  • Physics-informed modeling

    Physics-informed kernel methods, Gaussian process regression, Bayesian manifold regularization, deep learning, and sparsity-promoting methods.

  • Statistical theory

    Asymptotic, non-asymptotic, and high-dimensional theory for data-driven and physics-informed modeling, control, and decision-making.

  • Algorithms exploiting rank-structured matrices

    Efficient computation for data-driven and physics-informed modeling, control, and decision-making with semiseparable, hierarchical off-diagonal low-rank (HODLR), hierarchical semiseparable (HSS), and sequentially semiseparable (SSS) matrices.

  • Control

    Applications in robotic control, motion control, and process control.

  • Spatiotemporal data modeling

    Modeling large datasets that vary across space and time.

Publications and grants

He has published over 120 peer-reviewed publications, including nearly 40 papers in Automatica and IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control.

He coauthored Regularized System Identification: Learning Dynamic Models from Data and was one of four plenary speakers at the 19th IFAC Symposium on System Identification in Padova, Italy, in 2021.

He has participated in research projects in Sweden, elsewhere in Europe, and in China. As principal investigator, he has received CNY 10.0 million and SEK 3.6 million in research grants.
