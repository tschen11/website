Tianshi Chen

Tianshi Chen is a professor in the School of Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen). He received his PhD in Automation and Computer-Aided Engineering from The Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2008 and worked in the Division of Automatic Control at Linköping University before joining CUHK-Shenzhen in 2015.

His research focuses on data-driven and physics-informed methods for modeling, control, and decision-making, with applications in high-performance control and spatiotemporal data modeling.
